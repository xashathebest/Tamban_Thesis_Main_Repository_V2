# Native anatomical association audit

This audit addresses software ownership decisions, using unchanged Model 1 and Model 2 detections. The engineering thresholds remain containment 0.80, association score 0.70, competing-parent margin 0.12, center padding 0.05, anatomical alignment 0.18, and maximum longitudinal span 1.35. The detector confidence thresholds, class mapping, NMS, model files, and grading weights were not changed by this association repair.

## Reproduced defects and corrections

1. **An ineligible alternative vetoed a valid Head.** For Head `(70,30,110,50)` and parents `1:(0,0,120,80)`, `2:(78.5,0,198.5,80)`, parent 1 scores 0.7542 with full containment. Parent 2 scores 0.6394 with only 0.7875 containment, failing both configured acceptance gates. Previously its 0.1148 score difference nevertheless produced ambiguity. Only eligible parents now compete for ownership; every failed alternative remains in diagnostics with its failed gates. This correction does not lower any threshold.

2. **Global matching could borrow a neighbor's duplicate Head.** Two Heads inside two overlapping parent rectangles both strongly favor parent 1 when its supplied reliable polygon supports them (approximately 0.973 versus 0.744). The old total-score solver assigned the second Head to parent 2 to increase the total matching score. Its reported margin still described parent 1's advantage, rather than support for the selected parent. Global one-to-one selection now uses only independently supported ownership edges. It chooses one detection per region and parent, preserving the strongest raw confidence followed by association support and deterministic geometry ties. A duplicate stays unassigned instead of moving to its second-choice fish. Ambiguous detections do not reserve a parent from a clear detection.

3. **An inconsistent optional Tail invalidated an independently supported required Head.** With a distinct Head and Body and a Tail detection collapsed onto the Head, the previous anatomical check marked both Head and Tail ambiguous. The required-region gate then caused Ungraded. Distinct required Head/Body evidence is now preserved and only the conflicting optional Tail is withheld. A Head/Tail conflict without independent Body support remains ambiguous. A supplied reliable axis also checks transverse Head/Body overlap before treating that pair as independent.

4. **A guessed AABB axis was used as a reliable anatomical direction.** For a near-square parent box, physically separated diagonal or vertical parts could project into the same horizontal segment, or put the Body outside the projected Head–Tail interval. An AABB major axis is now explicitly a hint. Native two-dimensional collapsed-anatomy checks remain active; projection/order hard gates require an explicitly reliable supplied axis. `ParentAnchor.orientation_reliable=False` allows a candidate PCA orientation to be displayed without enabling those hard gates. An axis never establishes ownership by itself, and no head-versus-tail direction is inferred.

5. **Unassigned parts lacked an explanation.** Every unassigned raw part now has an explicit reason, including no visible parent, invalid geometry, insufficient containment, outside center, insufficient score, or duplicate region. Raw confidence, native box, all parent scores, and all threshold failures remain available.

6. **Analysis-crop clipping was mislabeled as original-image clipping.** `grading_engine.evaluate` treated `parent_crop_clipped` and `crop_clipped` as `out_of_frame`; aggregation also matched every rejected reason containing `clipp`. Crop-only loss therefore produced `UG_OUT_OF_FRAME` even when native image contact was never reported. `_clipping_state` now separates these diagnostics and reports `PARENT_CROP_CLIPPED` for crop-only loss. Missing required parts still produce Ungraded. Explicit native-image clipping and rejected `frame_clipped` gates remain conservative. An explicit `parent_frame_clipped` value overrides legacy bbox-contact `frame_clipped`: `None` records unknown native silhouette clipping and `False` allows source-bound native evidence to disprove it. Explicit `out_of_frame=True` remains authoritative.

7. **Reviewed native masks lost their boundary provenance.** `experimental_segmentation.prepare` constructed reviewed candidates with only a frame key, while the validator's native boundary waiver recognized MobileSAM provenance only. A fully reviewed silhouette strictly inside the original frame was consequently rejected solely because its parent rectangle included edge background. Reviewed candidates now retain native shape, coordinate space, parent ID, original parent box, frame key, and reviewer provenance. The validator verifies these fields before allowing the reviewed full-frame foreground to disprove bbox-only clipping. Actual mask foreground touching the image edge still fails; missing reviewer proof, wrong coordinates, shape, parent, box, or frame cannot bypass validation. Engineering consistency never independently sets ownership approval or grading approval.

8. **Reviewed refinement bypassed global anatomical uniqueness.** Explicit reviewed labels run after geometric one-to-one selection. Two reviewed Head detections for one fish, or a reviewed Head moved onto a fish with another selected Head, could both become assigned. The post-refinement pass now withholds the entire conflicting fish/region group as `reviewed_region_ownership_conflict`. Detector confidence and review-label order never break this contradiction. Separate regions and separate fish retain their valid assignments, and original detections remain intact. The initial six-case regression produced three failures before this correction.

## Diagnostic contract

Existing fields remain unchanged. Each serialized decision adds `candidate_parent_ids`, `acceptance_thresholds`, and `anatomical_checks`. Each candidate adds:

- `eligible` and `failed_checks` with the unchanged acceptance thresholds.
- `orientation`: native axis, source, reliability, normalized longitudinal and transverse positions, and `head_tail_direction_known:false`.
- `mask`: whether a polygon was supplied, center support, provenance, and `role:supporting_geometry_only`.

Association is a native-frame, frameless geometry function. Actual frame dimensions, frame identity, crop bounds, and frame-border clipping belong to the calling inference layer; crop contact must not be reported as original-image clipping. SAM output or a high SAM score alone does not supply reviewed ownership. Automatic candidate masks must pass their independent engineering checks and retain their unvalidated status; only approved caller-supplied geometry may enable reliable-axis gates.

## Validation and limits

`tests/test_association_additional_regressions.py` reproduces the defects with exact native-coordinate software fixtures, replays all seven historical CSV records without inventing detections, checks parent/part ordering invariance, preserves competing-fish ambiguity, and verifies that an unvalidated axis cannot activate hard gates. Before the repair its initial 12 cases produced **9 failures and 3 passes**. The existing association, optional-Tail, mask-ownership, two-model pipeline, body-width integration, fish-geometry integration, and runtime-diagnostic suites passed together with the new regressions: **147 tests and 36 subtests**, in 6.46 seconds on CPU.

After the boundary and reviewed-label corrections, the combined association, grading, verdict, optional-Tail, mask-ownership, real backend-provenance, experimental segmentation, reviewed-feature runtime, geometry, and runtime-diagnostic suites passed **262 tests and 41 subtests in 9.03 seconds on CPU**. New coverage resides in `test_additional_boundary_semantics.py`, `test_additional_reviewed_boundary.py`, and `test_additional_reviewed_uniqueness.py`. These are software fixtures with explicit synthetic review provenance; they do not supply scientific calibration or expert ownership labels for real images.

Fixed-detection real-image comparison is recorded separately in `results/qa/additional/association_comparison.json`. The seven-record historical CSV replays 22 native Model 2 candidates; its accepted Head/Body/Tail counts remain 2/4/0, with five fish Ungraded. On current fixed detections for 17 dataset images, accepted Head/Body/Tail counts changed from 18/67/5 to 24/71/10 and Ungraded from 108/117 to 105/117. On ten stress scenarios (56 parents), accepted counts changed from 8/34/0 to 10/35/3 and Ungraded from 52/56 to 51/56. These are association coverage measurements, not measured accuracy gains: there are no independently reviewed parent-to-part ownership labels for these inputs. Increased grading coverage alone does not establish correct attribution.

The score formula still reserves support for a reliable instance mask. Some edge Heads satisfy 0.80 containment but fail the unchanged 0.70 score gate. This is now explicit rather than hidden. No absent detection, calibration threshold, expert ownership label, occlusion, or missing anatomy was fabricated. Genuine overlapping-parent ambiguity, absent required parts, weak detector evidence, and unvalidated masks can therefore still produce Ungraded.
